# Screenplay Breakdown — in your browser

The app starts by itself and opens in a new browser tab.

- If no tab opened: click the **PORTS** tab at the bottom of this screen, then the globe icon next to
  **Screenplay Breakdown (8000)**.
- Pop-up blocked? Allow pop-ups for github.dev / app.github.dev, or use the PORTS tab.
- Only you can open the link (you must be signed in to GitHub).
- Your projects stay in this codespace until you delete it. To come back later, open
  https://github.com/codespaces and click this codespace.
- When you are done for the day, close the tab; GitHub stops the codespace after 30 minutes of inactivity
  (your projects are kept).
